Why Subwords Matter

  • 1. Handles Rare Words: If a model sees an unusual word like "unbelievability", it doesn't fail. It splits it into un + believ + ability.
  • 2. Compact Vocabulary: Instead of memorizing millions of distinct words, the model only needs 32,000 to 100,000 subwords to represent any word in human language!
  • 3. Punctuation & Spaces: Spaces (shown here as ␣) and punctuation are also tokens, which is why prompts consume tokens even for spaces.
💡 Next Step: Once tokens are converted into numbers (Token IDs), the model looks up each ID in a massive table called an Embedding Matrix to get its coordinates in 3D word space.
