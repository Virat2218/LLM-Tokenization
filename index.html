Why Subwords Matter in LLMs

  • 1. Handles Any Word: A model doesn't need to know every rare word in advance. A word like "unfaithfulness" is simply split into un + faith + fulness.
  • 2. Fixed Vocabulary Size: Models keep their vocabulary between 32,000 and 100,000 subwords to cover entire languages, code, and symbols efficiently.
  • 3. Spaces & Punctuation: Spaces (shown here as ␣) and commas are individual tokens, which is why prompts consume token quota even for formatting.
💡 From Tokens to Vectors: Each token ID acts as an index into the model's Embedding Table, transforming the integer into high-dimensional coordinates in semantic space!
