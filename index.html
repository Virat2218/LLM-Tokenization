Sentences vs. Words in 3D Space

  • 1. Compositionality: A sentence embedding is formed by pooling or contextualizing all its token vectors through transformer attention layers.
  • 2. Shared Space: In modern embedding models (e.g., Sentence-BERT), sentences about dogs cluster near the word dog, while sentences about coding cluster near algorithm.
  • 3. Geometry: Sentences appear as distinct diamond beacons in our 3D space, connected to their nearest word and sentence neighbors.
